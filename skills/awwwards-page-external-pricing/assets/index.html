Keep the boundaries visible

An estimate, with its workings.

Monthly compute = requests × active seconds × allocated resource rates. Each monthly resource subtotal rounds once to the nearest cent, half up; the rounded subtotals, storage and workspace fee are then added. Displayed rate units never determine the calculation.

Runtime accepts milliseconds. The first 10 GB of storage are free; storage rounds once to cents. No tax, transfer, idle time, discounts, commitments or currency conversion is included. A custom workspace fee is unknown, so its grand total stays unknown. These choices describe this original demo only.

Questions / plain answers

Before you continue.

Does changing the display unit change my total?

No. Per-hour display is the per-second rate multiplied by 3,600. The workload remains measured in active seconds.

Does no compute usage mean no monthly cost?

No. A workspace fee or billable storage can remain. The estimate shows each component separately.

Can I purchase or request a real quote here?

No. This is a local interface study with fictional rates, hardware and plans. The review dialog does not submit information.
